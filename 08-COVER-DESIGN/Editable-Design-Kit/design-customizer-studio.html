A. Monday Block 1 Thumbnail & Title Pairing Checklist

  • ☑ Rule 01 (3-Word Max): Primary thumbnail headline contains 3 to 5 high-contrast words maximum so mobile viewers parse it in < 1.5 seconds.
  • ☑ Rule 02 (Zero Text Duplication): Your 52–68 character video title complements the thumbnail hook rather than repeating the exact same phrase.
  • ☑ Rule 03 (Quantified Contrast): Include at least one concrete number, dollar figure, timeframe, or Before-vs-After split (`35 Hrs -> 4 Hrs`).
  • ☑ Rule 04 (WCAG AAA Legibility): Maintain >= 7.0:1 luminance contrast between foreground typography (`#FFFFFF` / `#2DD4BF`) and background (`#0F172A`).
  • ☑ Rule 05 (Right-Bottom Timestamp Safe Zone): Never place critical words in the bottom-right 140x48px corner where YouTube overlays the video duration badge.
  • ☑ Rule 06 (A/B Backup Ready): Export two distinct thumbnail variants (`Variant A: Math Contrast` and `Variant B: Status-Quo Warning`) before hitting record on Wednesday.

## Section 7: Deliverability Shield & 60-Day Cold-List Hygiene SOP

Unopened emails destroy your sender reputation with Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo, dragging warm buyers into the Promotions or Spam tab. Enforce this technical and behavioral hygiene checklist every month (Reference Prompt `P40` in Chapter 8).

### 7.1 Technical DNS & Inbox Placement Verification

- [ ] **SPF Record Verified:** Sending domain DNS contains a valid `v=spf1` TXT record authorizing your ESP.
- [ ] **DKIM Signature Active:** 2048-bit DKIM cryptographic key is verified and passing in ESP domain settings.
- [ ] **DMARC Policy Published:** `_dmarc` TXT record is live (`v=DMARC1; p=quarantine;` or `p=reject;`) with alignment passing.
- [ ] **Plain-Text Formatting Enforced:** Welcome emails contain zero heavy hero banners, zero multi-column HTML tables, and maximum 1–2 links per email.
- [ ] **Email 1 Reply Trigger Active:** Email 1 explicitly invites a 1-word or 1-sentence reply to establish bilateral contact affinity with inbox providers.

### 7.2 Automated 60-Day Cold Subscriber Pruning Rule

* **ESP Segment Filter Query:** `Subscriber has been on list >= 60 days AND Total Opens in last 60 days == 0 AND Total Clicks in last 60 days == 0`

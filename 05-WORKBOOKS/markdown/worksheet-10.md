## Section 2: Contract Protection & Native Integration Storyboard Checklist

### 2.1 Mandatory Sponsorship Contract Redline Audit

Verify every sponsorship agreement against these 6 non-negotiable protection rules before signing:

- [ ] **Payment Schedule Locked:** `50%` non-refundable deposit invoiced upon contract signature; remaining `50%` due Net-15 upon content publication (never accept Net-60 or Net-90 agency terms without a `+15%` financing surcharge).
- [ ] **Revision Cap Enforced:** Maximum **1 factual accuracy revision pass** on the script/outline prior to filming; zero re-shoots for subjective stylistic preferences after script approval.
- [ ] **Usage Rights Time-Boxed:** All paid ad whitelisting, Spark Ads, and raw footage rights explicitly state a start date and expiration date (zero free perpetual licensing).
- [ ] **Exclusivity Narrowly Defined:** Any exclusivity clause lists specific named competing products (`Max 3–5 domains`) and is restricted strictly to the agreed 30/60/90-day window.
- [ ] **Kill Fee Included:** If the sponsor cancels the campaign after script approval or filming, the `50%` deposit is retained in full as a production kill fee.
- [ ] **Conversion Tracking Configured:** Dedicated UTM link (`?utm_source=creator_name&utm_medium=sponsorship`) and custom short redirect link are tested prior to launch.

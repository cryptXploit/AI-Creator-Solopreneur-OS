To drop your weekly studio hours from $H_{\text{total}}$ down to `6.0 hours` without losing revenue, apply the following mandatory classification rules to every platform and task in your inventory:

1. **LOCK AS PRIMARY PILLAR (Select Exactly 1):** The single deep-dive channel where you build maximum authority and trust (either *Long-Form YouTube Video* OR *Flagship Deep-Dive Written Newsletter*).
2. **SYSTEMATIZE AS WATERFALL CHANNEL (Select Maximum 2):** The top two secondary networks that accept automated/prompt-extracted derivatives from your Pillar transcript and drive $\ge 80\%$ of your social profile clicks.
3. **AUTOMATE VIA TEMPLATE / AI PIPELINE:** Tasks that must never be performed manually from scratch again (e.g., silence cutting, transcript-to-thread drafting, carousel layout population, welcome email delivery).

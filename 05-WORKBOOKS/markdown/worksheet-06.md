## Section 3: Platform-Native Constraint Verification Audit ($\text{PNFW}_i$ Gate)

An unadapted cross-post receives a **Platform-Native Format Weight ($\text{PNFW}_i$)** of `0.20` (watermarked/link-throttled) or `0.50` (generic copy-paste), destroying algorithmic reach. Verify all 10 platform-native translation constraints below to lock $\text{PNFW}_i = 1.00$ across all 15 derivatives:

- [ ] **1. Zero Cross-Platform Watermarks (`Assets 01–03`):** All 3 vertical video clips were exported clean from the NLE timeline with zero TikTok, Reels, or CapCut watermarks.
- [ ] **2. 9:16 UI Safe-Zone Compliance (`Assets 01–03`):** All burned-in kinetic captions and diagrams sit strictly inside the central `1080x1320` safe zone (clear of the top 15% header and bottom 25% caption/scrub-bar overlays).
- [ ] **3. Syntactic Loop Closure (`Assets 01–03`):** The final spoken clause of each Short connects grammatically into the `00:00` opening hook word without an awkward fade-to-black pause.
- [ ] **4. Carousel Slide Word-Count Ceiling (`Assets 04–05`):** Every slide in both `1080x1350` (`4:5`) PDF carousels contains $\le 28\text{ words}$ in `24pt+` high-contrast typography (`#0F172A` / `#4F46E5`).
- [ ] **5. Thread Standalone Post Integrity (`Assets 06–07`):** Every post in both X/Threads storms is $\le 260\text{ characters}$, contains zero inline hashtags in Post #1, and includes a visual architecture graphic in Post #3 or #4.
- [ ] **6. LinkedIn "See More" Cliffhanger Fold (`Assets 08–10`):** The first 2 lines of all 3 LinkedIn text posts total `150–210 characters` and end with an unresolved tension hook before the fold break.
- [ ] **7. Zero External Links in Social Feed Bodies (`Assets 04–13`):** Verified `0` raw `http://` or `https://` links inside the primary body text of LinkedIn posts, X Post #1, or Instagram captions (preventing the `-40%` to `-60%` algorithmic reach penalty).
- [ ] **8. First-Comment & DM Keyword Routing Active (`Assets 01–13`):** All conversion bridges on rented social feeds route via automated DM keyword triggers (`[TRIGGER_KEYWORD]`) or delayed first-comment links.
- [ ] **9. Newsletter Scannability & 3-Link Architecture (`Asset 14`):** Email issue uses bold subheaders every `250 words`, includes at least 1 embedded diagram/table, and contains 3 UTM-tagged links (`?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email`).

## Section 4: The 25-Point Anti-Slop & Corporate Cliché Inspection Checklist (Prompt `P25`)

Every uncalibrated AI cliché or corporate filler phrase left in your script increases your **Slop Penalty Factor ($S_{\text{pen}} = 1 + 0.15 N_{\text{slop}}$)**, reducing your composite $\text{SRVAS}$ score by `15%` per violation and signaling low-trust synthesis to sophisticated audiences. Run a `Ctrl+F` pass and **Prompt `P25`** across your manuscript to verify zero occurrences of all 25 high-friction slop patterns:

### Category A: Studio Zero-Tolerance Banned AI Cliché Tokens (Items 01–10)
- [ ] **01. Zero Pseudo-Profound Opening Transitions:** Verified `0` instances of generic "fast-paced world" or "landscape" throat-clearing openings.
- [ ] **02. Zero Overused Metaphorical Nouns:** Verified `0` instances of ornate literary nouns (weavings, lighthouses, musical orchestras, or kingdoms/domains).
- [ ] **03. Zero Hyperbolic Marketing Verbs:** Verified `0` instances of exaggerated rocket/charging verbs or "lock/key" metaphors (replaced with *Activate*, *Access*, *Deploy*, or *Execute*).
- [ ] **04. Zero Pompous Corporate Verbs:** Verified `0` instances of multi-syllable corporate substitutes for simple verbs (always use *use*, *apply*, or *build*).
- [ ] **05. Zero AI Digging Verbs:** Verified `0` instances of cliché exploratory verbs (replaced with *examine*, *audit*, *inspect*, or *break down*).
- [ ] **06. Zero Formulaic Transition Signposts:** Verified `0` instances of *"Furthermore," "Moreover,"* or *"Additionally"* at the start of spoken sentences.
- [ ] **07. Zero Moralizing Reminder Clauses:** Verified `0` instances of *"It is crucial to note," "Remember that,"* or *"Keep in mind."*
- [ ] **08. Zero Summary Telegraphing Phrases:** Verified `0` instances of *"To sum up," "Wrapping up," "All in all,"* or *"At the end of the day."*
- [ ] **09. Zero False-Enthusiasm Intro Cues:** Verified `0` instances of *"Buckle up," "Get ready,"* or *"Let's jump right in."*
- [ ] **10. Zero Symmetrical "Not Only / But Also" Overuse:** Verified maximum `1` instance per `2,000 words` of *"It is not just X, it is Y"* rhetorical contrast.

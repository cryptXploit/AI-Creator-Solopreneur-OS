### Script `S01`: Sponsor Asking for "Free Trial / Exposure / Affiliate-Only" Counter-Offer Script

* **SCRIPT ID:** `S01`
* **SCENARIO TRIGGER:** A SaaS, AI, or tech brand emails you asking for a dedicated video integration or newsletter feature in exchange for a "free Pro account" and a `20%–30%` affiliate commission link with `$0` upfront production fee.
* **PSYCHOLOGICAL MECHANISM:** *Risk-Sharing Reversal & Media Inventory Valuation.* Politely reframes your content slots as scarce, high-production-cost B2B media inventory while offering a hybrid structure where the brand proves skin in the game via a fixed base production fee.
* **EXACT COPY-PASTE TEMPLATE:**
```text
Subject: Re: [Brand Name] x [Your Channel/Newsletter Name] Partnership

Hi [Contact First Name],

Thanks for reaching out—I'm a fan of what your team is building with [Specific Brand Feature], and it's a strong workflow fit for the [Audience ICP, e.g., 14,500+ B2B operators and engineers] who watch/read [Channel/Newsletter Name].

Because our production team caps sponsored integrations at 1 partner per weekly sprint—and invests 4+ hours scripting and recording live workspace demonstrations rather than generic ad reads—we don't take on unpaid or commission-only placements.

However, when a tool is a direct fit for an upcoming tutorial, we do offer a **Hybrid Performance Structure** so your team gets guaranteed multi-channel reach plus performance upside:

1. **Guaranteed Base Production & Placement Fee ($[Base Fee, e.g., $1,250]):** Covers a 60-second live screen-demo integration inside our upcoming breakdown on *[Upcoming Validated Topic]* (YouTube + [X,XXX]-reader Newsletter + pinned UTM short-link).
2. **Performance Revenue Share ([20%–30%] Recurring Affiliate Tier):** Wired to our custom tracking redirect (`[yourdomain.com/brand]`) for all conversions beyond the initial cohort.

If your team has Q[1/2/3/4] partner budget to cover the $[Base Fee] base placement, I'd be glad to lock in the [Date] slot and send over the 1-page spec. Let me know how that sounds!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Title & Media Kit URL]
```
* **CUSTOMIZATION NOTES:** Set `[Base Fee]` at $75\%$ to $100\%$ of your Option 1 Single-Sprint Test Rate ($R_{\text{sponsor}}$). Over `30%` of SaaS affiliate managers have discretionary partner budgets they only release when a creator professionally declines `$0` upfront deals.
